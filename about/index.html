About me

The messier the system, the more I enjoy designing for it.

Most of my work lives where people rarely look: admin consoles, data pipelines, service tools. These are products that people use eight hours a day, so small frustrations add up fast. I untangle the workflow first, keep the tables organised, and only then make it look good. When it works, nobody notices the design. They just get their work done.
